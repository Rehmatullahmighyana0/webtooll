Frequently Asked Questions

Who invented Morse Code?

Morse code was developed in the 1830s and 1840s by Samuel Morse and Alfred Vail for use with the electrical telegraph, revolutionizing long-distance telecommunications.

What is the international distress signal in Morse code?

The universal distress signal is SOS: ... --- ... (three short dits, three long dahs, three short dits), chosen for its unmistakable distinctive rhythmic pattern.
