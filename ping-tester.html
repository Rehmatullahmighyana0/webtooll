Understanding Ping, Latency & Jitter

Ping measures the round-trip time (in milliseconds) required for a data packet to travel from your web browser to a destination server and back. Latencies under 50ms provide a snappy, seamless browsing and gaming experience, while delays above 150ms indicate packet routing congestion.
