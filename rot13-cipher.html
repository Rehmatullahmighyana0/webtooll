Understanding ROT13 and Caesar Substitution Ciphers

ROT13 is a simple reciprocal substitution cipher that replaces each letter with the 13th letter following it in the Latin alphabet. Because the alphabet contains 26 letters, applying ROT13 twice restores the original text. It is often used on forums, puzzle games, and spoilers.
