What is a Certificate Signing Request (CSR)?

A CSR is an encoded block of text submitted to a Certificate Authority (CA) like Let's Encrypt, DigiCert, or Sectigo when applying for an SSL certificate. It contains your domain name, organization info, and the public key corresponding to your private key.
