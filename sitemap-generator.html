Why Sitemaps Are Essential for Google Search Indexing

An XML sitemap acts as a roadmap for Googlebot and Bingbot, listing all valuable pages on your website so search engine spiders can discover and crawl newly published content immediately. Once downloaded, place sitemap.xml in your website root and submit the URL in Google Search Console.
