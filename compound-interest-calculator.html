The Exponential Power of Compound Interest

Compound interest has famously been called the eighth wonder of the world. Because interest generates its own interest, your money compounds exponentially over extended time horizons. A regular monthly investment of $500 growing at an 8% annual stock index return accumulates over $340,000 in 20 years, with interest accounting for over 60% of the balance.
