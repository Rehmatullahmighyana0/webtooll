Understanding Digital Color Models (HEX, RGB & HSL)

Whether you are designing a website, building an iOS/Android application, or preparing marketing assets, utilizing the correct digital color representation is crucial for design fidelity.

Color Models Explained

  • HEX (#RRGGBB): A 6-character hexadecimal code representing Red, Green, and Blue intensity from 00 (0) to FF (255). Standard for HTML and CSS.
  • RGB (rgb(r, g, b)): Represents additive colors via red, green, and blue light components ranging from 0 to 255.
  • HSL (hsl(h, s, l)): Represents Hue (angle on the 360° color wheel), Saturation (percentage), and Lightness (percentage). Favored by UI designers because tinting and shading are intuitive.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) require a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal body text and 3:1 for large text (AA compliance) to ensure readability for visually impaired users.
Color harmonies are mathematically calculated relationships on the 360-degree color wheel. Complementary colors sit 180° apart, while triadic colors form an equilateral triangle (120° apart).
