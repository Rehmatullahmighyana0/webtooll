How Chronological Age Is Accurately Computed

Because months possess variable lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and leap years occur every four years, simple division by 365 produces fractional inaccuracy. Our calculator tracks exact calendar month boundaries to give you precision down to the day.
