Frequently Asked Questions

How many total TCP/UDP ports exist?

A port number is an unsigned 16-bit integer, meaning there are 65,536 total possible port numbers ranging from 0 to 65535.

What is the difference between TCP and UDP?

TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) is connection-oriented and guarantees ordered delivery of packets. UDP (User Datagram Protocol) is connectionless and prioritizes low latency speed over packet verification.
