Best Practices for robots.txt Configuration

The robots.txt file sits in the root directory of your domain (e.g., https://example.com/robots.txt) to inform web robots about which pages they may or may not crawl. It protects server resources from being overwhelmed and prevents search engines from indexing sensitive backend or internal paths.
