Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum contrast ratio for WCAG AA compliance?

WCAG AA requires a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal body text (under 18pt or bold under 14pt) and 3:1 for large text (18pt and larger, or bold 14pt and larger) and non-text UI components.

How is the contrast ratio calculated?

The contrast ratio is calculated using relative luminance: (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), where L1 is the relative luminance of the lighter color and L2 is the relative luminance of the darker color, yielding values from 1:1 up to 21:1.
