Understanding Regular Expressions in Web Development

Regular expressions (regex) are pattern-matching formulas used in string validation (such as email addresses, phone numbers, and strong password checks) and data scraping. Testing patterns client-side helps you fine-tune greediness, capture groups, and boundary assertions without runtime exceptions.
