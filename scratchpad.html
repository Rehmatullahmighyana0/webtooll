Frequently Asked Questions

Will my notes disappear if I close my browser tab?

No. Every keystroke is saved immediately into your browser's persistent local storage. Your notes will be right where you left them when you reopen the page.

Can anyone else on the network view my scratchpad?

No. Since data is saved exclusively inside your browser's private client-side storage, no server or network intermediary can access it.
