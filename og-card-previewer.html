The Importance of Open Graph (OG) Meta Tags

Without Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:image), platforms like Facebook, Discord, and LinkedIn randomly choose an image and snippet from your page, resulting in cropped logos, low click-through rates, and broken branding.
