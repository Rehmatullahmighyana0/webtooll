The Mechanics of Glassmorphism

      
Glassmorphism relies on two fundamental CSS properties: backdrop-filter: blur(12px), which diffuses elements behind the container, and translucent RGBA backgrounds with delicate white borders (border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)) that emulate the beveled edges of physical glass.
