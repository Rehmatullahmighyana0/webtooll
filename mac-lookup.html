Frequently Asked Questions

How can I find my computer's MAC address?

On Windows, open PowerShell and run getmac or ipconfig /all. On macOS and Linux, run ifconfig or ip link in the Terminal.

Why does my smartphone show a randomized MAC address?

Modern iOS and Android operating systems use Private MAC Address randomization when connecting to Wi-Fi networks to prevent physical location tracking.
