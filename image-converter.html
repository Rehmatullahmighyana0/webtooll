Why Convert Images to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides superior lossless and lossy compression for web images. WebP images are typically 26% smaller than PNGs and 25-34% smaller than comparable JPEGs at equivalent quality, boosting Google PageSpeed Insights scores.
