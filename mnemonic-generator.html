Understanding the BIP39 Word Standard

Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 39 (BIP39) describes the implementation of a mnemonic sentence—a group of easy-to-remember words—for the generation of deterministic keys. The official BIP39 English dictionary contains exactly 2,048 carefully curated words where the first 4 letters uniquely identify each word.
