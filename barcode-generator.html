Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between UPC and EAN barcodes?

UPC (Universal Product Code) is a 12-digit format predominantly used in North America, while EAN (European Article Number) has 13 digits and is recognized globally.

Can I print barcodes generated by this tool on physical products?

Yes. The exported SVG is a crisp vector graphic that scales to any DPI without blurring, making it suitable for industrial thermal label printers and product packaging.
