Random Passwords vs Memorable Passphrases vs Custom Words

Modern cybersecurity emphasizes length over extreme complexity. A 16-character passphrase composed of four dictionary words with numbers (like Falcon-Galaxy-Echo-92) has over 70 bits of entropy and takes supercomputers trillions of years to brute force, while being vastly easier for human memory than a random string like x&9K#pL!2.
