How to Calculate Percentages in Everyday Life

Percentages represent fractions out of 100 and are commonly used for calculating discounts, sales taxes, tips at restaurants, and investment returns.

Real-World Percentage Examples

  • Shopping Discounts: If an item costs $80 with a 25% discount, you calculate 25% of 80 = $20 off, making the final price $60.
  • Salary & Price Increases: If a price rises from $50 to $65, the change is (65 - 50) / 50 = +30% increase.
  • Grade & Score Percentages: If a student scores 42 out of 50 on an exam, the grade is (42 / 50) × 100 = 84%.
