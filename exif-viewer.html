Understanding Photographic EXIF Metadata & Privacy Risks

Exchangeable Image File Format (EXIF) metadata is automatically recorded by digital cameras and smartphones whenever a picture is captured. It frequently includes sensitive privacy details such as exact GPS longitude/latitude coordinates of your home, camera serial numbers, and exact timestamps. Scrubbing metadata protects your physical privacy before sharing photos on social networks.
