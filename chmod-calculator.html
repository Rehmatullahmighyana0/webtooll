Understanding Linux Chmod Octal Permissions

In UNIX and Linux systems, file permissions are assigned to three scopes: the file's Owner, the group owner Group, and all Others. Each permission is represented by an octal digit calculated by summing:

  • 4 for Read access (r)
  • 2 for Write access (w)
  • 1 for Execute access (x)
Hence, 7 (4+2+1) grants full read/write/execute rights, and 5 (4+1) grants read and execute only.
