Why Is Tracking Word and Character Count Critical?

Whether you are crafting an academic essay, preparing social media copy, or writing blog posts for search engine optimization (SEO), adhering to character and word length limits is essential:

  • Twitter / X Posts: Maximum 280 characters per tweet.
  • Google Meta Descriptions: Ideal length between 150 and 160 characters to avoid truncation in Google Search SERP snippets.
  • LinkedIn Posts: Up to 3,000 characters, with optimal engagement around 150 to 300 words.
  • Amazon Product Titles: Typically limited to 200 characters.
