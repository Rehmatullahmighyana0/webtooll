How Does the Luhn Mod-10 Algorithm Work?

The Luhn algorithm doubles every second digit from right to left, subtracting 9 if the result exceeds 9. The sum of all digits must be a multiple of 10. Major payment card networks (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) strictly use this standard to catch typos and fraud.
