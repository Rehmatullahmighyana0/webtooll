Why Use an Online Character Counter?

Whether you are drafting a tweet for Twitter / X, composing a mobile SMS notification, optimizing an SEO meta tag for Google search, or formatting your Instagram profile biography, keeping strictly within character restrictions is vital. Frequently Asked Questions

Does this counter count spaces and punctuation?

Yes. The tool gives you both the total character count (including spaces and punctuation) and the count strictly without whitespace.

How are UTF-8 bytes calculated?

Standard ASCII English characters take 1 byte each, while emojis and accented characters may occupy 2 to 4 bytes under UTF-8 encoding.
