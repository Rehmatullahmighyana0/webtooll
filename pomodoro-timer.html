Frequently Asked Questions

Will the timer alarm sound if I minimize my browser?

Yes. The timer sounds an audible synthesizer chime when your countdown finishes, and your browser tab title updates continuously with remaining minutes.

Can I change the session durations?

Default standard cycles are 25 minutes for work, 5 minutes for short breaks, and 15 minutes for long breaks.
